Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

SGC Uploader is a personal tool operated and used only by its owner (SennaGamingYT).

Data we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name) and permission to upload videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts, and read-only statistics of the owner's own account and videos (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.list, user.info.stats).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel, and read-only access to the owner's own channel and video statistics and YouTube Analytics (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly). Statistics are used only to show the owner how their own videos perform.

How data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload videos the owner approved.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • No data about other TikTok or YouTube users is collected.

Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the owner's computer until deleted or revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or in the Google Account (Security → Third-party access).

Contact the owner via the email listed on the SennaGamingYT channel.
