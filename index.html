SGC Uploader

A personal upload tool for the SennaGamingYT channel.

SGC Uploader is a private tool used by one creator (SennaGamingYT) to publish their own Honor of Kings gameplay highlights. Highlights are edited on the creator's own computer; the creator reviews every video, title and caption and approves each upload manually.

With the creator's permission (OAuth), the tool sends an approved video to the creator's own TikTok inbox as a draft and to their own YouTube channel. The creator finishes and publishes TikTok drafts in the TikTok app. The tool is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Uploads videos the creator made and approved, to the creator's own accounts only.
  • Does not read, collect or post anything from other users.
  • Does not like, follow, comment or message.

Contact

For questions, contact the creator via the email listed on the SennaGamingYT channel.
